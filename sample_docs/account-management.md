# Northwind Supply — Account Management

## Opening a trade account

Trade accounts are available to VAT-registered businesses. Apply at
northwind.example/trade with your company registration number and two trade
references. Applications are assessed within 3 working days.

Approved accounts start on a £2,500 credit limit with 30-day terms. Limits are
reviewed every 6 months based on payment history.

## Payment terms

Invoices are issued on the day of dispatch and are due 30 days from the end of
the month of invoice. Late payment interest is charged at 4% above base rate.

Accounts more than 14 days overdue are placed on stop, meaning new orders are
held until the balance clears.

## Pricing and discounts

Trade pricing is applied automatically at checkout once logged in. Volume
discounts start at 5% on orders over £1,000 and rise to 12% on orders over
£10,000. Discounts do not apply to clearance items.

## Users and permissions

Account administrators can add unlimited users. Each user is set as either a
Buyer, who can place orders up to a per-order limit set by the administrator,
or a Viewer, who can see order history and invoices but cannot place orders.

## Closing an account

Email accounts@northwind.example from the registered administrator address.
Outstanding balances must be cleared before closure. Order history remains
available for download for 90 days after closure.
