# Northwind Supply — Shipping Guide

## Delivery options

Standard delivery is 3 to 5 working days and costs £4.95, free on orders over
£75. Express delivery is next working day if ordered before 14:00 and costs
£11.50. Saturday delivery costs £18.00 and must be selected at checkout.

We deliver to mainland UK addresses. Deliveries to the Scottish Highlands,
Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands take 2 additional
working days and are not eligible for express delivery.

## Tracking

Every dispatched order receives a tracking number by email. The carrier is
Parcelforce for standard delivery and DPD for express. Tracking information
becomes active once the carrier scans the parcel, usually by 20:00 on the day
of dispatch.

## Missed deliveries

DPD attempts delivery twice. After a second failed attempt the parcel is held
at the nearest pickup point for 7 days. Parcelforce leaves a card and holds
the parcel at the local depot for 14 days.

## Heavy and oversized items

Orders over 30kg ship on a pallet and are booked as a two-person delivery.
Pallet deliveries are kerbside only — the driver cannot carry items inside the
building. Lead time for pallet delivery is 5 to 7 working days and a contact
telephone number is mandatory at checkout.

## International

We ship to the EU, the United States and Australia. International orders are
sent DDU, meaning import duty and local taxes are payable by the recipient on
arrival. International delivery takes 7 to 14 working days.
