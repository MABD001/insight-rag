# Northwind Supply — Returns and Refunds Policy

## Eligibility

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery provided they are unused and
in their original packaging. Custom-fabricated parts, cut cable and clearance
items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.

Items damaged in transit must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Send
photographs of the packaging and the damaged item to support@northwind.example
and we will arrange a replacement at no cost.

## Starting a return

Log in to your account and open the order, then choose "Return items". You will
receive a return authorisation number (RMA) by email within one business day.
Write the RMA number clearly on the outside of the parcel. Parcels received
without an RMA number are held for 14 days and then returned to sender.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 business
days of the warehouse receiving the item. Bank processing may add a further 3
to 5 days depending on your card issuer.

Original delivery charges are refunded only when the return is the result of an
error on our side, such as a mis-picked or faulty item. Return shipping is paid
by the customer for change-of-mind returns.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and
place a new order. This keeps stock allocation accurate and is usually faster
than waiting for an exchange to clear.

## Business accounts

Account customers on 30-day terms receive refunds as a credit note against the
account rather than a payment reversal. Contact your account manager if you
need a cash refund instead.
