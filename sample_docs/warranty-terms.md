# Northwind Supply — Warranty Terms

## Standard warranty

All power tools carry a 24-month warranty against manufacturing defects from
the date of invoice. Hand tools carry a lifetime warranty. Consumables such as
drill bits, blades, abrasives and batteries are excluded.

Batteries carry a separate 12-month warranty covering capacity loss beyond 30%
of rated capacity under normal use.

## Registering an extended warranty

Registering a power tool within 28 days of purchase extends the warranty to 36
months at no charge. Registration is done at northwind.example/register and
requires the serial number printed on the tool's rating plate.

## What is not covered

Wear from normal use, damage caused by using the wrong consumable, corrosion
from outdoor storage, and any tool that has been opened or modified by anyone
other than an approved service centre.

Tools used on hire or on a commercial site are covered for 12 months rather
than 24. This is stated on the invoice at the point of sale.

## Making a claim

Claims are made through your account under "Warranty claims". You will need the
serial number, the invoice number and a description of the fault. Approved
claims are repaired or replaced within 10 working days of the tool arriving at
the service centre.

Error code SB-4021 on the digital torque wrench range indicates a failed
calibration sensor and is covered under the standard warranty. Do not attempt
to reset the tool; return it for service.
